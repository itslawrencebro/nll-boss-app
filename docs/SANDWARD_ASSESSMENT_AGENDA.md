# Sandward Trucking — 30-Minute Operations Assessment Agenda

> **INTERNAL DISCOVERY PREP ONLY — DO NOT PUBLISH ON PUBLIC PAGE**
> Prospect: **Sandward Trucking** (Fort Worth, TX)
> Target Window: **Wednesday, Nov 18 or Thursday, Nov 19 (Afternoon Central)**
> Status: Discovery opportunity (not a signed customer; do not send calendar invitations without Sean's approval)

---

## Session Objective
Trace Sandward Trucking's live freight workflow from load booking to final payment, identify the single highest-friction manual bottleneck, quantify weekly staff hours and cash-flow lag tied to it, and agree on a clear, low-friction next step.

---

## 30-Minute Structured Agenda

### 00:00 – 03:00 | Context & Baseline Profile
- Confirm fleet / brokerage mix (asset trucks vs. brokered loads)
- Confirm **monthly load volume** and current dispatch / back-office headcount
- Confirm **current TMS**, document storage (email/Drive/desktop), and **accounting software** (e.g., QuickBooks)

### 03:00 – 15:00 | End-to-End Workflow Walkthrough
Walk step-by-step through one recent standard load and one messy/exception load across all 6 stages:
1. **Load Booking**: How does a tender / rate confirmation arrive, and who enters it into the TMS?
2. **Dispatch**: How are drivers/carriers assigned, confirmed, and given load instructions?
3. **Tracking**: How many manual check calls happen per load, and how are delays flagged?
4. **POD Collection**: How does the signed BOL/POD get back to the office (driver photo, text, email, portal), and how often is it missing or illegible?
5. **Invoice Generation**: What triggers invoicing, who verifies accessorials (detention/lumper/layover), and how is data moved into accounting?
6. **Payment & Reconciliation**: Average days from delivery → POD verified → invoice sent → cash collected.

### 15:00 – 22:00 | Manual Work & Top Bottleneck Isolation
- Ask: *"Which single handoff creates the most chasing, rework, or delayed billing every week?"*
- Quantify rough weekly friction:
  - Hours spent chasing PODs or re-keying load details
  - Number of delivered loads waiting on paperwork before billing
  - Frequency of missed accessorial charges or late customer updates

### 22:00 – 27:00 | Practical Fix Options (Without Replacing Current TMS)
- Outline 1–2 targeted workflow improvements (e.g., structured POD intake + exception queue, or verified POD → QuickBooks invoice readiness handoff)
- Clarify what stays untouched (their existing TMS and accounting ledger)

### 27:00 – 30:00 | Agreed Next Step
- Confirm whether Sandward wants a scoped **Phase 1 Workflow Design & Implementation Proposal** targeting their #1 bottleneck
- Agree on decision-maker review date and any follow-up sample screenshots/SOPs (remind not to send sensitive financial/tax docs unless under NDA)
